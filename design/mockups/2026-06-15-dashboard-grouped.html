Direction B — platform-grouped sections. Each platform owns a banded section with its own accent stripe, icon, transport line ("via VMentory Agent" / "via REST + SSH"), and a per-platform capability legend (the green/struck chips). This makes the providers' differing powers explicit at the section level rather than per-row — an operator immediately sees Proxmox can't be a migration source and Hyper-V has no live CPU%.
• Grouping = natural cluster home. The PVE cluster tag lives in the section header; nodes nest under it without a per-row cluster column.
• Capability legend ≠ per-row gating. The legend is informational; verbs are still gated per-row (disabled Migrate on PVE).
• Trade-off vs Direction A: grouping is great when platforms behave differently, but it prevents a single global sort/filter across all hosts and uses more vertical space. Cross-platform questions ("which host is hottest, any platform?") are harder here than in the single list.
