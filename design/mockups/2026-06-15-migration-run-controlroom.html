Direction B — control room for a resumable job. The migration is modelled as a persisted DAG of steps (left rail: done ✓ / running / failed ✕ / pending), not a one-shot wizard. This is the screen you live on for an 18-minute, failure-prone run.
• Partial failure is first-class. The job stops at the failed step, keeps everything completed before it, and offers per-step recovery: edit input & retry · retry as-is · skip · roll back & abort. Idempotency + reserved-VMID state make "retry" safe.
• Rollback safety is always visible. "Source intact" lives in the top banner and the rollback note spells out exactly what a rollback destroys (only the half-built target) and what it never touches (Hyper-V source).
• Live + persisted logs. Per-step log streams over SSE (reusing EventHub) and is persisted, so reopening the job after a browser refresh — or days later — shows the same history. Resumable means you can close the tab and the job survives.
